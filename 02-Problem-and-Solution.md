# 02. Problem Statement and Solution

## Problem Statement

In today's digital world, finding skilled and reliable people for specific tasks can be challenging. Employers and clients may find it difficult to judge a person's actual abilities based only on a resume, qualifications, or self-reported experience.

At the same time, skilled individuals may struggle to prove their abilities and find suitable opportunities, especially when they have limited professional experience.

## Challenges Identified

- **Difficulty verifying skills:** Claims about skills and experience can be difficult to evaluate.
- **Lack of practical evidence:** Traditional profiles may not adequately demonstrate a person's actual capabilities.
- **Hiring uncertainty:** Employers may struggle to identify candidates who match their requirements.
- **Limited opportunities:** People with practical skills may find it difficult to showcase their work effectively.

## Proposed Solution

SKILLCHECK aims to provide a platform where individuals can showcase their skills and experience, while employers can discover potential workers according to their requirements.

The platform is designed around two main user groups:

1. **Workers or applicants:** People who want to present their skills, experience, and professional capabilities.
2. **Employers or hirers:** People or organizations looking for workers with suitable skills.

By bringing these two groups together, SKILLCHECK aims to make discovering and evaluating talent more straightforward.

## How SKILLCHECK Helps

- Provides a platform for workers to present their professional profiles.
- Helps employers discover people with relevant skills.
- Aims to improve transparency between workers and potential employers.
- Encourages skill-based evaluation rather than relying only on qualifications.

## Expected Outcome

SKILLCHECK aims to reduce uncertainty during talent discovery, help individuals present their abilities more effectively, and make it easier for employers to find suitable candidates.

## Conclusion

SKILLCHECK focuses on connecting people who have skills with people who need those skills. Its long-term goal is to support a more transparent and accessible skill-based hiring process.
