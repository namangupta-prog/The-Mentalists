## 2. Expected Impact
SKILLCHECK aims to:
- Improve the visibility of skilled workers.
- Help employers discover potential candidates.
- Encourage skill-based hiring.
- Make professional abilities easier to present.
- Support connections between workers and employers.

## 3. Future Scope
Potential improvements include:
- Verified worker profiles.
- Work portfolios and practical skill evidence.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Advanced search and filtering.
- Improved communication between workers and employers.
- Secure payment integration, if required.
- Better mobile accessibility.

These are potential enhancements, not claims about existing functionality.

## 4. Acknowledgements
We thank the hackathon organizers for the opportunity to develop SKILLCHECK and Base44 for providing the platform used to build and host the prototype.

## Conclusion
SKILLCHECK aims to create a more accessible way for workers to showcase their capabilities and for employers to discover suitable talent.
