# 03. Objectives and Features

## 1. Project Objectives

The main objective of SKILLCHECK is to connect skilled individuals with employers who are looking for workers with suitable skills and experience.

The project aims to:

- Help workers showcase their skills and experience.
- Help employers discover potential candidates.
- Encourage skill-based hiring.
- Make professional information easier to understand.
- Provide a user-friendly experience for both workers and employers.

## 2. Key Features

### 2.1 Two User Types

SKILLCHECK is designed for two main types of users:

- **Workers or Applicants:** Individuals who want to showcase their skills, experience, and professional abilities.
- **Employers or Hirers:** Individuals or organizations looking for workers with relevant skills.

### 2.2 Worker Profiles

Worker profiles can help applicants present information about their skills, experience, and professional background.

### 2.3 Talent Discovery

The platform aims to help employers identify potential workers based on their skills and experience.

### 2.4 User-Friendly Interface

The website aims to provide a clear and accessible interface that allows users to navigate the platform easily.

### 2.5 Theme Selection

The planned theme options include Default, Dark, and White, allowing users to choose their preferred website appearance.

## 3. Benefits of SKILLCHECK

- Helps individuals present their abilities.
- Makes it easier for employers to discover potential talent.
- Encourages a focus on practical skills.
- Supports connections between workers and hirers.
- Aims to make the hiring process more accessible.

## 4. Future Enhancements

Potential future improvements include:

- Skill assessments and verification.
- Work samples and project portfolios.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Secure online payments.
- Advanced search and filtering.
- Improved communication between workers and employers.

These enhancements are proposed ideas and should not be considered implemented unless they are available in the current application.

## 5. Conclusion

SKILLCHECK aims to make skill-based hiring more accessible by helping workers present their capabilities and helping employers discover suitable candidates. Its goal is to create a platform where skills and experience play an important role in connecting people with opportunities.
