# 01. Project Overview

## Project Name
**SKILLCHECK — Your Work Speaks.**

## About the Project
SKILLCHECK is a web-based project designed to help people demonstrate their skills through practical work and evidence of what they can do. The goal is to focus on demonstrated abilities rather than relying only on claims or traditional descriptions of skills.

The project is being developed using Base44, with the application available online.

## Problem Statement
People may find it difficult to demonstrate their real abilities clearly. A list of skills on a profile does not always show how well someone can apply those skills in practice. This can make it harder for others to understand a person's capabilities.

## Proposed Solution
SKILLCHECK aims to provide a platform where users can present evidence of their skills and work. By focusing on practical demonstrations, the project seeks to make skills easier to understand and evaluate.

## Project Goal
The main goal of SKILLCHECK is to make skill demonstration more practical, clear, and accessible.
